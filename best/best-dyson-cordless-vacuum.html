The Dyson V15 Detect Absolute is the Dyson cordless vacuum that makes the most sense for most UK homes. Buying guide: how to choose the right Dyson cordless vacuum

Which Dyson is which?

Dyson's numbers mostly follow age and power. The V8 (130 AW) and Cyclone V10 (150 AW) are older and cheaper. The V11 (185 AW) adds an LCD screen and automatic power. The V12 Detect Slim and V15 Detect add a laser head that lights up dust and a particle sensor. The Gen5detect adds HEPA filtration to 0.1 microns, and the V16 Piston Animal is the newest flagship at 315 AW. The PencilVac is a separate ultra-light line for quick cleans.

Absolute, Advanced, Animal and Extra

The second part of the name tells you what is in the box, not a different motor. Absolute bundles usually include more floor heads, such as the Laser Slim Fluffy or soft roller head, plus extra tools. Advanced and Animal versions focus on carpets and pet hair with a de-tangling Motorbar. Extra bundles add HEPA filtration or extra tools. Always compare the box contents, as the same model can cost very different amounts depending on the bundle.

Run time and battery

Dyson quotes run time in Eco mode with a non-motorised tool, so expect much less on Boost or with the Motorbar on carpet: often 15 to 25 minutes. The V8 manages up to 40 minutes, most others 60, and the V16 and Gen5detect up to 70. The V12 and V15 have a battery that clicks out, so a spare doubles the time for larger homes.

Weight matters more than you think

A cordless vacuum hangs from your wrist, so every 500 grams counts on stairs and ceilings. The PencilVac weighs about 1.8 kg and the V12 Detect Slim 2.2 kg, while the V15, Gen5detect and V16 weigh 3.1 to 3.5 kg. If you live in a flat or have wrist or shoulder problems, the V12 is usually the better buy than a more powerful but heavier model.

Is the laser head worth it?

The Laser Slim Fluffy head on the V12 and V15 projects a line of light across hard floors, which shows fine dust, crumbs and pet hair you would otherwise miss. Most owners say they clean more thoroughly with it. On carpet it adds nothing, so if your home is mostly carpeted, the V11 Advanced or V16 Piston Animal with a strong Motorbar matter more.

Watch out for imports and renewed units

Some Dyson listings on Amazon.co.uk are renewed machines or imports with an EU charger. Check that the listing is new, sold or dispatched by Amazon or Dyson, and comes with a UK plug and the two-year Dyson warranty. Prices also move a lot around Black Friday, so compare before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

Which Dyson cordless vacuum is best?

For most homes the Dyson V15 Detect Absolute, which combines 240 AW of suction, a laser head that shows dust and up to 60 minutes of run time for around £499.

What is the best value Dyson cordless vacuum?

The V12 Detect Slim Absolute. It has the laser head, particle sensor and LCD screen of the V15 but weighs only 2.2 kg and usually costs around £80 less.

What is the cheapest Dyson cordless vacuum worth buying?

The Dyson V8 Advanced at around £249. It has a de-tangling head and weighs 2.5 kg, but runs for up to 40 minutes and has a small bin.

Is the Dyson V16 worth the extra money?

Only if you have a large home with deep carpets or pets. It has 315 AW and up to 70 minutes, but the V15 Detect Absolute covers most homes for around £220 less.

Which Dyson is best for allergies?

The Gen5detect, with sealed HEPA filtration that captures 99.99% of particles down to 0.1 microns. The V16 Piston Animal also has fully sealed filtration.

Is a Dyson better than a Shark?

Not always. Dyson leads on suction, filtration and features, while Shark often offers self-emptying docks and anti hair wrap for less. Our main cordless vacuum guide compares both.
