CATAN has been a family favourite for decades, and the 6th Edition is the version now sold on Amazon.co.uk. Three or four players build roads, settlements and cities on an island made of hexagonal tiles, and the first to reach 10 victory points wins. Because the board is laid out differently every time, no two games are the same. A game takes around 60 minutes, and the box says ages 10 and up.

The charm is in the trading. You will always have too much wool and not enough brick, so everyone is bargaining all game long. Buyers say it is easy to explain after one round and that it gets teenagers off their phones. If you have a bigger group, note that the 5 to 6 player extension is sold separately and needs this base game. Prices on Amazon move between about £32 and £37, which is fair for a game that gets played for years.

Buying guide: how to choose the right Christmas gifts under £50

Think about who it is for

A good Christmas gift fits something the person already enjoys. A board game suits a family that plays together, a candle or bath set suits someone who loves a cosy evening, and a coffee lover will use a moka pot every morning. A gift for a hobby they do not have yet often ends up in a cupboard.

Set a budget per person

With a big family, costs add up quickly. Decide in advance how much you spend per person, for example £15 for colleagues and Secret Santa and up to £50 for your partner or parents. Everything in this list stays under £50, and the Baylis & Harding set and Wasgij puzzle are well below £20.

Gifts to share

Christmas is about being together, so a gift the whole household can use often goes down best. A board game like CATAN, a card game like Exploding Kittens or a jigsaw puzzle gives you something to do on Christmas Day and Boxing Day, when everyone is at home.

Order in time

Amazon.co.uk is busy in December. Most Prime orders arrive within a day or two, but popular items such as LEGO sets and certain Jellycat colours can sell out. Order before mid December to be safe and check the delivery date on the product page before you pay.

Black Friday and price swings

Black Friday falls on 27 November 2026 and Cyber Monday on 30 November. Amazon devices like the Echo Pop and big brands such as LEGO are often discounted then. Prices in this guide are typical street prices from early November and can change daily, so check the current price before you buy.

Check returns and gift receipts

Most items bought on Amazon.co.uk between early November and the end of December can be returned until the end of January. You can add a gift receipt at checkout, so the recipient can swap a colour or size without seeing what you paid.
