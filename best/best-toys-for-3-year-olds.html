Buying guide: how to choose the right toys for 3 year olds

Always check the age on the box

In the UK, toys must carry an age warning if they contain small parts that are unsafe for under 3s. Everything in this guide is labelled ages 3+ by the manufacturer. A 3+ toy can still be a choking risk for a baby or toddler sibling, so keep small parts out of their reach.

What a 3 year old enjoys

At three, most children love pretend play, building and simple games with rules. They copy what adults do, like shopping and cooking, and act out scenes from favourite shows. Toys that support that, such as play food, a playset or a first board game, tend to get played with for months rather than days.

Open ended toys last longest

Toys without a fixed end, like DUPLO, magnetic tiles and wooden play food, can be played with in a different way every day. They also grow with your child, so a 3 year old builds a simple wall and a 6 year old builds a whole castle. That makes the higher price of sets like CONNETIX easier to justify.

Screens, batteries and extra costs

Many parents prefer screen free toys for a three year old. An audio player like the Yoto Mini plays stories without a screen, but you pay extra for cards. The VTech camera needs four AA batteries and Color Wonder markers need special paper. Check what is not in the box so the present works on the day.

Christmas and Black Friday

Black Friday falls on 27 November 2026, and LEGO, PAW Patrol and Bluey toys are often discounted then. Popular sets can sell out in December, so order before mid December to be safe. Prices in this guide are typical Amazon.co.uk prices from early November and can change daily.
