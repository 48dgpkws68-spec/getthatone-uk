The 10 best cordless vacuum cleaners of 2026

A good cordless vacuum cleaner has replaced the corded hoover in many UK homes. Run time, bin size, weight and pet hair performance vary a lot, though. We compared the cordless vacuums on Amazon.co.uk on these points, on price and on thousands of UK buyer reviews. Buying guide: how to choose the right best cordless vacuum cleaner UK

Run time: what you really need

Manufacturers quote run time on the lowest power setting without a motorised head. Expect roughly half that in normal use with the floor head. For a typical three-bedroom UK house, 40 minutes of quoted run time is usually enough. Models with removable batteries or two batteries in the box give you more flexibility.

Pet hair and anti-tangle brushes

Long human hair and pet fur wrap around brush rolls and cut performance. Shark's Anti Hair Wrap, Dyson's comb vanes and Dreame's cutting blades all reduce this. If you have a dog or cat, an anti-tangle brush and a mini motorised tool for sofas and stairs are worth paying for.

Bagged, bagless or self-emptying?

Most cordless vacuums are bagless and you empty the bin over the rubbish bin, which can release dust. Bagged models like the Henry Quick keep dust contained but cost more to run. Self-emptying docks from Shark and Samsung empty the bin for you and are the cleanest solution, at a higher price.

Weight and balance

Most cordless vacuums weigh 2.5 to 3.5 kg. The motor and bin usually sit near the handle, which can make them feel top-heavy when cleaning high up. If you have stairs or a bad back, try to choose a lighter model and check that the handheld converts easily.

Can a cordless replace a corded vacuum?

For most homes, yes. The top models from Shark, Dyson and Samsung have suction comparable to a good corded hoover. Very large houses with deep carpets may still benefit from a corded upright or a second battery.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best cordless vacuum cleaner in the UK?

The Shark PowerDetect Clean & Empty IP3251UKT is the best all-rounder for around £449. The Dyson Gen5detect is more powerful but costs more, and the Henry Quick is the best value.

Is Shark or Dyson better?

Dyson has slightly stronger suction and lighter machines. Shark has better anti-tangle brushes, the Flexology wand and self-emptying options at lower prices. For pet owners on a budget, Shark is often the better buy.

How long do cordless vacuum batteries last?

Typically three to five years before run time drops noticeably. Many models, including Dyson, Shark and Dreame, have replaceable batteries.

Are cordless vacuums good on carpet?

The best ones are. Look for a motorised brush head and strong suction, such as the Dyson V15 or Shark Stratos. Budget models work well on low-pile carpet but struggle with thick rugs.

Which cordless vacuum is best for allergies?

A model with whole-machine HEPA filtration, such as the Dyson Gen5detect or V15, or a bagged model like the Henry Quick, keeps fine dust contained best.
