The 10 best cordless vacuum cleaners of 2026

Short answer (reference prices as of 26 November 2026): our top pick is the Shark PowerDetect Clean & Empty IP3251UKT at approx. £369 on Amazon. Best value: Numatic Henry Quick HQ100 at approx. £199. Cheapest in this top 10: Vax ONEPWR Blade 4 Pet CLSV-B4KP at approx. £169.

A good cordless vacuum cleaner has replaced the corded hoover in many UK homes. There is no cable to drag from room to room, it converts into a handheld for stairs and the car, and the best models clean carpets as well as a traditional upright. Run time, bin size, weight and pet hair performance vary a lot, though. We compared the cordless vacuums on Amazon.co.uk on these points, on price and on UK buyer reviews. These are the ten best.

How to choose in 5 questions

Do you have pets?

Long pet hair wraps around brushrolls, so look for an anti-tangle head. No. 1 Shark PowerDetect at £369 and No. 5 Shark Stratos at £269 have Anti Hair Wrap Plus and a motorised pet tool. No. 9 Dreame R20 at £239 has an anti-tangle head and No. 10 Levoit at £179 an anti-hair wrap brush. No. 8 Vax ONEPWR Blade 4 Pet at £169 includes a motorised pet tool but has no anti-tangle brush.

Do you want to stop emptying the bin by hand?

No. 1 Shark PowerDetect at £369 empties into a 2 L dock that Shark says holds dirt for weeks, though the dock takes up floor space. No. 6 Samsung Bespoke Jet Lite empties into a 2 L Clean Station bag for the same price, £369. No. 2 Henry Quick at £199 compacts dust into sealed 1 L pods, so the dust stays contained, but the pods are an ongoing cost.

How big is your home?

In a larger home, run time counts. No. 9 Dreame R20 quotes up to 90 minutes, No. 1 Shark PowerDetect and No. 3 Dyson V16 up to 70, and No. 2 Henry Quick 70 on low with the brush head off. No. 8 Vax stops at 45 minutes. All of these are maximum figures at low power. In a flat with mostly hard floors, No. 10 Levoit at £179 with 60 minutes in Eco mode is enough.

How heavy can it be?

No. 6 Samsung Bespoke Jet Lite weighs 2.7 kg with its brush and has a handheld body of only 1.45 kg. No. 9 Dreame R20 is 2.74 kg and No. 10 Levoit 2.79 kg. No. 8 Vax is 3.1 kg and No. 2 Henry Quick 3.2 kg. The heaviest are No. 3 Dyson V16 at 3.5 kg, No. 7 Miele at about 3.6 kg, No. 1 Shark PowerDetect at 3.71 kg and No. 5 Shark Stratos, which Shark lists at 4.1 kg.

Mostly carpet or hard floors?

On carpet, No. 3 Dyson V16 has the highest suction figure in this list (315 AW) and No. 1 Shark PowerDetect detects the floor type and adjusts itself. The Levoit listing recommends hard floors and low pile carpet, so keep No. 10 for those. On hard floors, the laser on No. 4 Dyson V15 and the LED dust lights on No. 9 Dreame R20 show up fine dust.

When to pick No. 2 or 3?

No. 2: Numatic Henry Quick HQ100

Choose No. 2 Numatic Henry Quick instead of No. 1 Shark PowerDetect if you want dust-free emptying for less money. At £199 it costs £170 less, the 1 L pods keep dust sealed in and it is lighter at 3.2 kg versus 3.71 kg. You give up the self-emptying dock, the dirt sensors and the anti hair wrap brushroll, and the pods are an ongoing cost.

Verdict

Our conclusion

The Shark PowerDetect Clean & Empty is the best cordless vacuum for most UK homes: it detects dirt, edges and floor type, has an anti hair wrap brushroll and empties itself into a 2 litre dock, for around £369. The Henry Quick is the best value, with dust-free pod emptying from the maker of Henry for about £199. For the highest suction figure in this list, 315 air watts, and up to 70 minutes of run time, the Dyson V16 Piston Animal is the premium choice.

Runs each battery down on short-pile carpet at every power setting, measures suction with a vacuum gauge and spills 50 g of flour, 26 g of Cheerios and 5 g of pet hair on carpet and hard floor for a single pass. Its best overall is the Dyson V15 Detect Absolute (3 kg, 0.76 litre bin, 60 minutes; cons: heavy, expensive, no self-emptying). Its reviewers use every vacuum in their own homes on different floor types, pet hair and larger debris, and score cleaning, comfort, design and battery life. The Shark PowerDetect Cordless is its best for pet hair: it came top in every cleaning test (4.75 out of 5) and was the only model with 5 out of 5 in every pet hair test, but it was also the heaviest in the group and scored 2.33 out of 5 for comfort and manoeuvrability. Buys every vacuum it reviews and measures dust pick-up on carpets and hard floors, allergen retention and battery run time on low and maximum power. Buying guide: how to choose the right cordless vacuum cleaners

Run time: what you really need

Makers quote run time at the lowest power, often without a motorised head. Shark and Samsung say their 60 minutes apply in Eco or at minimum power with a non-motorised tool, and Numatic measures the Henry Quick's 70 minutes with the brush head off. Which? says some models overstate battery life by as much as a third, and calls 15 to 16 minutes on maximum power decent. The Henry Quick manages 16 minutes on high and TechRadar timed the Dreame R20 at seven minutes in Boost.

Pet hair and anti-tangle brushes

Long human hair and pet fur wrap around brush rolls and cut performance. Shark's Anti Hair Wrap Plus, the conical brush bars of the Dyson V16, the anti-tangle head of the Dreame R20 and Levoit's anti-hair wrap brush are all designed to prevent this. The Henry Quick and the Vax Blade 4 Pet have no such system. If you have a dog or cat, a mini motorised tool for sofas and stairs is worth having: Shark, Vax and Levoit include one.

Bagged, bagless or self-emptying?

Most cordless vacuums are bagless and you empty the bin over the rubbish bin, which can release dust. The Henry Quick compacts dirt into sealed 1 litre pods, which keeps dust contained but means buying pods. The docks of the Shark PowerDetect and the Samsung Bespoke Jet Lite empty the vacuum for you, into a 2 litre container and a 2 litre bag respectively. Which? puts the bin of a typical cordless at 0.5 to 1 litre.

Weight and noise

Which? says cordless vacuums weigh around 3 kg on average, against 6.4 kg for corded machines. In this list the weights run from 2.7 kg for the Samsung with its brush attached to 3.71 kg for the Shark PowerDetect and 4.1 kg for the Shark Stratos (Shark's figure for the IZ400UK). Noise figures on the Amazon.co.uk listings run from 72 dB for the Henry Quick to 87 dBA for the Samsung; Dyson and Shark give no noise figure for the V16, V15 and Stratos.

Charge time

Which? puts the average charge time at around three hours. The figures we found: 2 hours for the Dreame R20 (TechRadar's spec table), 2.5 hours for the Henry Quick, 3 hours for the Vax, 3.5 hours for the Samsung and the Levoit, and 4.5 hours for the Dyson V15. A removable battery, as on the Henry Quick, Shark Stratos, Samsung and Vax, lets you charge it away from the vacuum or buy a spare.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best cordless vacuum cleaner in the UK?

The Shark PowerDetect Clean & Empty IP3251UKT is the best all-rounder for around £369. The Dyson V16 Piston Animal has the highest suction figure (315 AW) but costs £719, and the Henry Quick is the best value at £199.

Is Shark or Dyson better?

It depends on what you value. Expert Reviews found the Shark PowerDetect went toe to toe with the Dyson V15 Detect on the share of spills it cleaned up, with slightly less suction, and it empties itself. The Dyson V15 is lighter (3.1 kg against 3.71 kg) and has the laser head. For pet owners, TechRadar names the Shark PowerDetect its best cordless for pet hair.

How long do cordless vacuum batteries last?

Makers rarely give a lifespan. Samsung says the battery of the Bespoke Jet Lite keeps 70 percent of its performance for up to 500 charge cycles. Shark and Samsung both give a 2 year warranty on the battery. The Henry Quick, Shark Stratos, Samsung, Miele and Vax have batteries you can take out and replace.

Are cordless vacuums good on carpet?

The better ones are. TechRadar gave the Shark PowerDetect top marks in every one of its medium-pile carpet tests, and Expert Reviews names the Dyson V15 Detect its best cordless overall. The Levoit LVAC-300 listing recommends hard floors and low pile carpet only.

Which cordless vacuum is best for allergies?

Look at how the dust is filtered and how you empty it. Miele claims 99.999 percent dust retention for the HEPA Lifetime Filter of the Triflex HX1 and Dyson 99.99 percent of particles down to 0.3 microns for the V15. The Henry Quick seals dust in pods, and the Shark PowerDetect and Samsung Bespoke Jet Lite empty into a closed dock.

What is the best cordless vacuum for stairs?

A light body and a handheld mode help most. The Samsung Bespoke Jet Lite at £369 has a handheld body of 1.45 kg, and the Miele Triflex HX1 at £349 switches between Comfort, Reach and Compact modes. The Shark PowerDetect at 3.71 kg is one of the heaviest to carry up.

What is the best cordless vacuum for pet hair on a budget?

The Vax ONEPWR Blade 4 Pet at £169 includes a motorised pet tool and has 2,418 ratings on Amazon.co.uk, but it has no anti-tangle brush and runs for 45 minutes at most. The Levoit LVAC-300 at £179 adds an anti-hair wrap brush and a mini motorised pet brush. For £239 the Dreame R20 has an anti-tangle head, 190 AW and up to 90 minutes.
