Buying guide: how to choose the right wireless earbuds

Match your earbuds to your phone

AirPods work best with iPhone, Galaxy Buds with Samsung phones and Pixel Buds with Pixel phones, as many smart features only work within the same brand. Sony, Bose, Soundcore and JLab work equally well on any phone. If you switch between platforms, choose a brand-neutral pair.

How good is noise cancelling?

Active noise cancelling (ANC) uses microphones to cancel out steady background noise like engines, trains and air conditioning. The best models from Sony, Bose and Apple remove most of it. Budget models reduce noise but less effectively. ANC is less effective against voices and sudden noises.

Battery life

Manufacturers quote battery life for the buds and in total with the case. Five to six hours per charge is fine for most commutes, eight hours or more suits long flights. Noise cancelling and spatial audio reduce battery life. Look for quick charging if you often forget to charge.

Fit and comfort

A good seal is essential for both bass and noise cancelling. Most earbuds come with several sizes of tip, and foam tips seal better than silicone. If in-ear tips feel uncomfortable, open-fit designs like the AirPods 4 are an alternative, at the cost of less noise isolation.

Codecs and sound quality

iPhones use AAC, while many Android phones support LDAC or aptX for higher quality. In practice the earbuds' tuning and fit matter more than the codec. Use the brand's app to adjust the equaliser, as default tuning is often bass-heavy.

Water resistance for sport

IPX4 means protection against sweat and splashes, enough for the gym and running in light rain. IP54 adds dust resistance, and IP57 means they survive a brief submersion. None of these earbuds are designed for swimming.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best wireless earbuds in the UK?

The Sony WF-1000XM6 are the best all-rounder for around £249. For iPhone users, the AirPods Pro 3 are a close alternative. On a budget, the Soundcore Liberty 4 NC offer great value at under £60.

Are AirPods good with Android phones?

They work, but you lose many features such as automatic switching, spatial audio settings and easy battery checks. Android users are better off with Sony, Soundcore, Pixel Buds or Galaxy Buds.

Which earbuds have the best noise cancelling?

The Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen) have the strongest noise cancelling, closely followed by the Sony WF-1000XM6 and the AirPods Pro 3.

Can I use wireless earbuds for running?

Yes. Choose a pair with at least IPX4 water resistance and a secure fit, such as the AirPods Pro 3, Pixel Buds Pro 2 or Galaxy Buds3 Pro.

How long do wireless earbuds last?

Typically two to four years. The batteries slowly lose capacity and cannot usually be replaced, so battery life will gradually shorten.

Which wireless earbuds have the longest battery life?

The Soundcore Liberty 4 NC (£59) lasts 8 hours per charge and 50 hours with the case. The JLab Go Air Pop (£19) gives 8 hours and 32 hours in total, and the Sony WF-C710N (£79) 8.5 hours in the buds and 30 hours with the case. The AirPods 4 with ANC (£149) are shortest, at 5 hours and 20 hours in total.

What are the best wireless earbuds under £100?

The Soundcore Liberty 4 NC (£59) gives the most for the money, with effective adaptive noise cancelling and 50 hours in total. The Sony WF-C710N (£79) has more balanced sound, and the Nothing Ear (a) (£69) adds LDAC and a transparent design. If you only need basic sound and no noise cancelling, the JLab Go Air Pop costs £19.
