Buying guide: how to choose the right 65-inch TV under £1000

What does £1000 buy in a 65-inch TV?

In 2026 a £1000 budget easily covers a 65-inch Mini LED TV with 144 Hz gaming, and most of the best picks cost £650 to £800. Mini LED sets such as the Hisense 65U7QTUK and TCL 65C6K-UK use many small LEDs that dim in zones, so HDR looks brighter and blacks deeper. Below £550 you mostly find QLED and QNED models without local dimming. A 65-inch OLED usually costs more than £1000 at regular prices; see our best OLED TV guide for those.

Is 65 inches the right size?

For a 65-inch 4K TV, a viewing distance of roughly 2 to 3 metres works well, which suits most UK living rooms. Measure the width too: most 65-inch TVs are about 145 cm wide, so check your TV unit or wall space before you order. If you sit closer or the room is small, our best 55-inch TV guide is the better starting point.

Mini LED, QLED or QNED?

Mini LED gives the most contrast and brightness under £1000 and is our first choice for a living room. QLED and LG's QNED add a colour layer for richer colour but usually have no local dimming, so dark scenes look greyer. A big screen makes that difference easier to see, which is why the Mini LED models rank highest here.

Refresh rate for gaming

With a PS5, Xbox Series X or gaming PC, look for a native 120 Hz or 144 Hz panel with VRR and ALLM. Here the Hisense 65U7QTUK, TCL 65C6K-UK, TCL 65T8C-UK and Samsung QE65QN70F qualify. The other TVs in this list run at 60 Hz, which limits consoles to 60 frames per second. A high motion rate on the box is not the same as a native refresh rate.

Dolby Vision or HDR10+?

Netflix, Disney+ and Apple TV+ mostly stream in Dolby Vision, while Prime Video also uses HDR10+. Hisense, TCL and Amazon support both. Samsung supports HDR10+ only, Sony supports Dolby Vision but not HDR10+, and the LG QNED models support neither. Brightness matters more than the format: a dim TV gets little out of either.

Budget for sound and prices

Even good TV speakers sound thin on a big screen. Most picks here cost well under £1000, so plan £150 to £300 for a soundbar. TV prices on Amazon.co.uk change often, especially around Black Friday, and some models here move by £100 or more. Check the current price before you buy.

What is the best 65-inch TV under £1000?

Should I buy a 55-inch or 65-inch TV?

If you sit around 2 metres or more from the screen, 65 inches usually feels right. For smaller rooms see our best 55-inch TV guide.
