Buying guide: how to choose the right cordless vacuums under £200

What does a budget cordless vacuum get you?

Under £200 you give up the extras of £400 machines: self-emptying docks, laser dust lights and the very highest suction. You still get a motorised floor head, a handheld mode and 40 to 65 minutes of quoted run time. For flats and average houses with mixed floors that is enough. Very thick carpets and large homes are where a dearer model still earns its price.

Under £100 or under £200?

Around £100, cheap cordless vacuums such as the Hoover HF1 Plus and Ultenic U10 handle hard floors, low carpet and daily crumbs, but have less suction and simpler tools. Between £150 and £200 you get better carpet cleaning, proven anti hair wrap systems from Shark, sturdier build and longer guarantees. If the vacuum will be your only one, the higher bracket is worth it.

Run time on a budget

Manufacturers quote run time on the lowest setting, often without the motorised head. Expect roughly half in normal use. A quoted 40 minutes covers a typical three-bedroom UK house. If you worry about running out, look at the Bosch Unlimited 6 with two batteries, or the Tineco Pure ONE S30 and Dreame R10 Pro with up to 65 minutes.

Cheap alternatives to Dyson

New Dyson cordless vacuums usually cost more than £200 on Amazon.co.uk, so they are not in this list. The closest budget alternatives are the Shark PowerPro for all-round cleaning and hair, the Tineco Pure ONE S30 for suction on paper and the Bosch Unlimited 6 for low weight. Avoid paying for a renewed premium model unless you are happy with a shorter warranty.

Pet hair and anti hair wrap

Hair that winds around the brush bar is the most common complaint about cheap cordless vacuums. Both Sharks, the Hoover HF1 Plus, the Tineco and the Dreame have brushes designed to prevent it. The Vax Blade 4 Pet has no anti-tangle brush but includes a motorised pet tool for sofas and stairs.

Check the price and the model code

Prices in this bracket move a lot: several vacuums here have a recommended price well above £200 and only qualify at their usual Amazon.co.uk price. Check the price on the day. Also buy the UK version, with a model code ending in UK or GB, so the charger has a three-pin UK plug and the UK guarantee applies.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best cordless vacuum under £200?

The Shark PowerPro IZ380UK at around £179 is the best all-rounder: it adjusts to the floor type, has an anti hair wrap brushroll and runs for up to 50 minutes. The bagged Henry Quick at £199 is the better choice for allergy sufferers.

What is the best cordless vacuum under £100?

The Hoover HF1 Plus at about £99 is our pick. It offers up to 45 minutes of run time, an anti hair wrap brush bar and LED lights, and more than 1,800 UK buyers rate it around 4.1 out of 5. The self-standing Ultenic U10 at £99.99 is the alternative.

Are cheap cordless vacuums any good?

For hard floors, low-pile carpet and everyday cleaning, yes. Budget cordless vacuums have less suction and smaller bins than premium models, so they struggle more with thick carpets and very large homes. Stick to known brands with plenty of UK buyer reviews.

Is there a Dyson cordless vacuum under £200?

New Dyson cordless vacuums usually cost more than £200 on Amazon.co.uk. The cheapest Dyson in our main guide to the best cordless vacuum cleaners is the V15 Detect at around £499. Under £200, the Shark PowerPro and Tineco Pure ONE S30 are the closest alternatives.

Is Shark or Vax better on a budget?

Shark has the edge for long hair and pets thanks to its Anti Hair Wrap brushroll and bending wand. The Vax Blade 4 Pet is about £10 cheaper than the Shark PowerPro, lighter at 2.8 kg and its battery works across the Vax ONEPWR range, but it has no anti-tangle brush.

How long does a budget cordless vacuum run?

Quoted run times in this list range from 40 to 65 minutes on the lowest setting. In normal use with the floor head, expect roughly half. On maximum power some models, such as the Vax Blade 4 Pet, last only around 10 minutes.

Which budget cordless vacuum is best for pet hair?

The Shark PowerPro IZ380UK at £179 and the Shark IZ202UK at £199 both have Anti Hair Wrap brushrolls. The Vax ONEPWR Blade 4 Pet at £169 has no anti-tangle brush but comes with a motorised pet tool for sofas, stairs and pet beds.

Is it worth spending more than £200?

Only if you have a large home, thick carpets or want a self-emptying dock. Models above £300 add stronger suction, bigger batteries and smarter sensors. For a flat or an average house with mixed floors, the vacuums in this list are enough.
