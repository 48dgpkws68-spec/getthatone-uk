Buying guide: how to choose the right tablet for kids

Check the age the manufacturer gives

Amazon labels its Fire Kids tablets for ages 3 to 7 and its Kids Pro tablets for ages 6 to 12. Samsung, Lenovo and Apple do not give an age for their normal tablets, because they are made for everyone. For a child under 7 a dedicated kids tablet is the easiest choice. From about 8 a normal tablet with good parental controls works well.

Kids tablet or normal tablet

A kids tablet such as the Fire HD 8 Kids comes ready to use: a thick case, a child interface, a year of ad-free content and a guarantee if it breaks. A normal tablet such as the Samsung Galaxy Tab A11+ or the iPad gives access to every app, including school apps and YouTube Kids, but you set up the controls yourself and buy a case separately.

Parental controls

All tablets in this guide let you set time limits and approve apps. Amazon uses the Parent Dashboard, Samsung has Samsung Kids plus Google Family Link, Lenovo relies on Family Link and Apple uses Screen Time with Family Sharing. Set up the controls before you wrap the tablet, so it is ready on Christmas morning.

Screen size and storage

A 7 or 8 inch screen is light and easy for small hands. A 10 or 11 inch screen is better for films, drawing and homework but heavier. Choose at least 32 GB of storage, or a model with a microSD slot, so there is room for games and downloaded shows for long journeys.

Durability and guarantee

Young children drop tablets. The Amazon Fire Kids tablets come with a 2-year worry-free guarantee: if the tablet breaks, Amazon replaces it. For Samsung, Lenovo and Apple tablets, buy a sturdy case with a handle and consider a screen protector.

Buying for Christmas

Tablets for children are among the best selling Christmas presents, and prices often drop around Black Friday. Order in good time, charge the tablet fully and create the child profile before you wrap it. Then your child can start playing straight away instead of waiting for updates.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best tablet for kids?

Based on specs and parent reviews, the Amazon Fire HD 8 Kids (2024) is the best tablet for most children aged 3 to 7. Amazon labels it for that age, and it comes with a Kid-Proof Case, a year of Amazon Kids+ and a 2-year worry-free guarantee for around £150. For older children the Apple iPad 11-inch (A16) is the premium choice.

What age is a kids tablet suitable for?

It depends on the model. Amazon labels the Fire Kids tablets for ages 3 to 7 and the Kids Pro tablets for ages 6 to 12. Samsung, Lenovo and Apple give no age for their normal tablets. In practice, most children get their first tablet between 3 and 6.

Is a tablet a good Christmas present for a child?

Yes, a tablet is one of the most popular Christmas presents for children in the UK. Choose a model that fits the age, set up parental controls before you wrap it and charge it fully, so your child can use it on Christmas morning. Prices often drop around Black Friday.

Can my child use YouTube Kids on a Fire tablet?

Fire tablets run Fire OS from the Amazon Appstore, not the Google Play Store, so some Google apps are missing. If YouTube Kids or specific school apps matter, choose an Android tablet such as the Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 or Lenovo Tab 10.1, or an iPad.

What is the cheapest good tablet for kids?

The Amazon Fire 7 Kids is the best value at about £110. Amazon labels it for ages 3 to 7, and you get a Kid-Proof Case, a year of Amazon Kids+ and a 2-year guarantee. The screen is less sharp than on the Fire HD 8 Kids.

Do I need Amazon Kids+ after the first year?

No. After the free year you can renew Amazon Kids+ as a monthly subscription or stop it. The tablet keeps working, and the Parent Dashboard and apps you added yourself stay available.
