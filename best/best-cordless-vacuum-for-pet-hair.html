Buying guide: how to choose the right cordless vacuums for pet hair

What makes a cordless vacuum good for pet hair?

Three things: a brush bar that does not clog with hair, enough suction and agitation to pull fur out of carpet pile, and a small motorised tool for sofas, stairs and pet beds. Every model here is sold by its maker as a pet or animal version, has an anti hair wrap brush or includes a pet tool. Ideally you get all three.

Anti hair wrap: how it works and who needs it

Long hair winds around a spinning brush bar and slows it down. Shark's Anti Hair Wrap uses a comb-like guard to strip hair off as the roller turns, Dyson's De-tangling Motorbar uses vanes inside the head and Hoover's Anti-Twist bar has an integrated comb. With a long-haired dog, or long human hair in the house as well, it is the single most useful feature.

Dog hair or cat hair, carpet or hard floor

Coarse dog hair on hard floors is easy and almost any model manages it. Fine cat hair on carpet is harder, because it weaves into the pile and needs a motorised brush and strong suction. For carpeted homes choose one of the Shark or Dyson models. For hard floors and furniture, a light model with a good pet tool is enough.

Filters, allergies and pet smells

Pet dander is finer than hair and passes through poor filters. Look for sealed filtration, such as Shark's Anti-Allergen Complete Seal or Dyson's whole-machine filtration, and wash the filters as often as the manual says, since fur and dander clog them quickly. A self-emptying base limits your contact with dust. Shark's Odour Neutraliser is aimed at the dog smell that vacuuming can stir up.

Run time and bin size with pets

Quoted run times apply to the lowest setting, often without the motorised head. Pet hair usually needs a higher setting, so expect roughly half. Fur is also bulky: a 0.5 litre bin can fill in one room during moulting season. A 0.7 litre bin, a second battery or a self-emptying dock makes daily vacuuming far less of a chore.

Stick vacuum or robot for pet hair?

A robot keeps daily shedding under control on open floors, but it cannot do stairs, sofas, the car or the dog bed. Many pet owners end up with both. If you only buy one, a cordless stick with a pet tool covers more of the places where hair actually collects. We cover robots separately in our guide to robot vacuums for pet hair.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best cordless vacuum for pet hair in the UK?

The Shark PowerDetect Clean & Empty IP3251UKT at around £369 is the best all-rounder: Anti Hair Wrap Plus, automatic suction boost and a self-emptying base. The Shark PowerPro Pet at £199 is the best value and the Dyson V16 Piston Animal at £719 is the most powerful.

Is Shark or Dyson better for pet hair?

Both work well. Shark gives you more pet features for the money: Anti Hair Wrap, a pet tool in the box, odour control on the Stratos and a self-emptying base on the PowerDetect, from £199 to £369. Dyson has stronger suction and sealed filtration on the V16 at £719, and the V8 Advanced at £269 is lighter than any Shark here.

What is the best cordless vacuum for dog hair?

For a heavy shedder such as a labrador or German shepherd, choose a model with an anti hair wrap brush and a large or self-emptying bin: the Shark PowerDetect at £369 or the Shark Stratos Pet Pro at £269. On a tighter budget, the Shark PowerPro Pet at £199 has the same kind of brushroll.

Which cordless vacuum is best for cat hair on carpet?

Fine cat hair works its way into carpet pile, so suction and a motorised brush matter most. The Dyson V16 Piston Animal has the strongest suction at 315 AW, and the Shark PowerDetect boosts suction automatically on carpet. For low-pile carpet in a flat, the Dyson V8 Advanced at £269 is enough.

Do anti hair wrap vacuums really work?

They greatly reduce tangling, according to UK buyer reviews, but they are not maintenance free. Very long hair, string and threads can still catch at the ends of the roller, so check the brush bar every few weeks. Models without it, such as the Vax ONEPWR Blade 4 Pet, need hair cut off the brush more often.

Which cordless vacuum is best for pet allergies?

Look for sealed filtration and as little contact with the dust as possible. The Dyson V16 Piston Animal has fully sealed five-stage filtration, the Shark PowerPro Pet has an Anti-Allergen Complete Seal and the Shark PowerDetect empties into a closed base. Wash or replace filters on schedule, because dander clogs them.

What is the best cheap cordless vacuum for pet hair?

The Hoover HF1 Plus Pet at £139 is the cheapest model here with both an anti hair wrap brush bar and a motorised pet tool. The Vax ONEPWR Blade 4 Pet and the AEG 7000 Animal at £169 each include a motorised pet tool but no anti-tangle floorhead. For £60 more than the Hoover, the Shark PowerPro Pet at £199 is the stronger cleaner.

How often should I clean the filter if I have pets?

More often than the manual's minimum. Fur and fine dander block filters faster than ordinary household dust, and a clogged filter cuts suction and can cause smells. As a rule of thumb, rinse washable filters about once a month in a pet home, let them dry for at least 24 hours and empty the bin after every use.
