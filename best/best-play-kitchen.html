Buying guide: how to choose the right play kitchen for kids

Check the age on the box

Every kitchen in this guide is labelled by the manufacturer for ages 3 and up, or has a warning that it is not suitable under 3. Play kitchens often have small parts such as knobs, ice cubes and play food. Most children play with a kitchen from 3 to about 7. For a toddler under 3, look at a separate toddler toy instead.

Measure your space first

Play kitchens range from compact sets of about 50 cm wide, like the Tender Leaf Home Kitchen, to wide units of 110 cm, like the KidKraft Large White Kitchen. Measure the corner where it will stand and remember that doors need room to open. A kitchen island like the KidKraft Artisan needs space on two sides.

Wood or plastic?

Wooden kitchens from KidKraft, Hape, Melissa & Doug and Tender Leaf feel sturdier and look calmer in a living room. Plastic kitchens like the Smoby Tefal Studio Bubble are lighter, cheaper to move around and easier to wipe clean. They often have more effects, such as bubbling water, but feel less solid.

Height and growing room

A counter height of 45 to 50 cm suits a 3 or 4 year old. Taller kitchens of 95 cm or more, such as the KidKraft 53369 or the Melissa & Doug Chef's Kitchen, stay fun until age 6 or 7. A very small kitchen is cheaper but will be outgrown sooner.

Accessories, lights and assembly

Many big kitchens come without pots or play food, so add £20 to £35 for an accessory set. Lights and sounds need batteries, which are rarely included. Assembly of a large wooden kitchen easily takes one to two hours with a screwdriver, so do not leave it until Christmas Eve.

When to buy

Black Friday falls on 27 November 2026, a good moment to look for deals on KidKraft and Hape. Popular kitchens can sell out before Christmas, so order before mid December and check the delivery date. Prices on Amazon.co.uk change daily, so the prices in this guide are a guide only.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best play kitchen for kids?

Based on specs and parent reviews, the KidKraft Red Vintage Kitchen 53173 is the best play kitchen for most families. KidKraft labels it for ages 3 and up, it has a fridge, oven, microwave and sink and costs around £100. The KidKraft Artisan Island 53441 is the best value at about £58.

What age is a play kitchen suitable for?

Most play kitchens are labelled by the manufacturer for ages 3 and up, because they contain small parts. All ten kitchens in this guide carry a 3+ label or a warning that they are not suitable under 3. Children usually play with a kitchen from 3 until around 6 or 7.

Is a play kitchen a good present for a 3 year old?

Yes. Age 3 is the typical starting age on the box, and children of this age love copying what they see in the real kitchen. A compact kitchen like the KidKraft Artisan Island or the Tender Leaf Home Kitchen suits a 3 year old well. Plan to add some pots and play food.

Is a wooden or plastic play kitchen better?

Wooden kitchens are sturdier and last longer, which suits families with more than one child. Plastic kitchens like the Smoby Tefal Studio Bubble are lighter and easier to clean and often have effects such as bubbling water. For long term use, most parents choose wood.

How big is a play kitchen?

Sizes vary a lot. The Tender Leaf Home Kitchen is 52 cm wide and 65 cm high, the KidKraft Red Vintage Kitchen about 84 cm wide and 91 cm high, and the KidKraft Large White Kitchen 111 cm wide and 112 cm high. Measure your space before ordering.

Are accessories included with a play kitchen?

Not always. The Tender Leaf Home Kitchen, Hape All-in-1 Kitchen and Smoby Tefal Studio Bubble include pots or crockery. KidKraft kitchens often come with only a toy phone, and Melissa & Doug sells accessories separately. Budget £20 to £35 for a pots and pans set.
