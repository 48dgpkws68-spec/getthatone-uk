Buying guide: how to choose the right 50-inch TV under £400

What does £400 buy in a 50-inch TV?

In 2026 a £400 budget covers almost every 50-inch LED and QLED TV on Amazon.co.uk, and even one Mini LED model, the TCL 50C6KS-UK. Around £300 to £400 you get quantum dot colour and usually Dolby Vision. Between £230 and £280 you find basic LED sets that are fine for a bedroom. What you do not get at this size and price is OLED or a native 120 Hz panel.

50 inch or 55 inch?

A 50-inch TV is roughly 112 cm wide, about 11 cm narrower than a 55-inch set, which matters in an alcove or on a small TV unit. The catch is choice: several of the best budget ranges, such as the 144 Hz TCL T8C and C6K, start at 55 inches. If you have the space, our guide to the best TV under £500 lists those larger models. If 50 inches is the limit, the ten TVs here are the ones to compare.

Mini LED, QLED or LED?

Mini LED gives the most contrast because the backlight dims in zones, and under £400 only the TCL 50C6KS-UK offers it at 50 inches. QLED and QNED add a colour layer for richer reds and greens but have no local dimming, so dark scenes look greyer. A standard LED TV such as the TCL 50V6C-UK or Hisense 50E6QTUK is the cheapest option and fine for the news, soaps and a second room.

Gaming on a 50-inch TV

Every TV in this list has a 60 Hz panel. That is fine for a Nintendo Switch and for most PS5 and Xbox games at 60 frames per second, but not for 120 Hz modes. Do not be misled by terms such as 120 Hz Game Accelerator in a product title: that is not the native refresh rate. For true 120 Hz or 144 Hz gaming you need a 55-inch model such as the TCL 55T8C-UK.

Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and smart platforms

TCL and Hisense support Dolby Vision, which Netflix and Disney+ use most. Samsung and Philips support HDR10+ instead, and the LG models only HDR10 and HLG. Google TV (TCL) has the biggest app choice, webOS (LG) and Tizen (Samsung) are the most polished, VIDAA (Hisense) includes Freely, and Titan OS (Philips) shows the most promotions. A streaming stick can always replace a weak platform.

Prices move around

TV prices on Amazon.co.uk change often, especially around Black Friday and in January. The Hisense 50E7STUK sits right at £399 and the TCL 50C6KS-UK has been listed up to £399, so both can cross the £400 line between sales. Check the current price before you buy. If a pick goes over budget, the TCL 50P7K-UK gives you most of the picture for around £300.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best 50-inch TV under £400?

The TCL 50C6KS-UK. It is the only 50-inch Mini LED TV in this budget and supports both Dolby Vision and HDR10+, for around £369 on Amazon.co.uk.

Is a 50-inch TV big enough for a living room?

Yes, for a viewing distance of roughly 1.5 to 2.5 metres. If you sit further away and have the width, a 55-inch TV from our guide to the best TV under £500 is worth considering.

Can I get a 50-inch OLED TV for under £400?

No. OLED TVs come in 48 inches rather than 50 and cost well above £400 from the major brands. Mini LED, as in the TCL 50C6KS-UK, is the closest alternative for contrast.

Which 50-inch TV under £400 is best for PS5 or Xbox?

All ten run at 60 Hz, so none supports 120 Hz modes. The TCL 50C6KS-UK has the best picture for games. For 144 Hz you need a 55-inch model such as the TCL 55T8C-UK.

What is the cheapest good 50-inch TV?

The TCL 50V6C-UK at around £229. It is a basic LED set, but it has Google TV with all the main UK apps and Chromecast built in.

Which brand has the best smart TV software at this price?

LG's webOS and Samsung's Tizen feel the most polished, and Google TV on the TCL models has the widest app choice. Hisense's VIDAA is quick and includes Freely.
