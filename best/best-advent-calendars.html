Buying guide: how to choose the right advent calendars

Decide who the calendar is for

The best advent calendar depends on the person, not the brand. Children usually want toys or chocolate, teenagers and adults often prefer beauty, tea or a puzzle calendar. Think about what they will still use after Christmas: LEGO and Playmobil sets stay in play, beauty products get used up, and chocolate disappears in December.

Check the age on the box

Toy calendars show the age the maker recommends, such as 4+, 5+ or 6+. Follow that age: it reflects how complex the builds are and whether small parts are safe. Every toy calendar in this list contains small parts and is not suitable for children under 3. Puzzle calendars like EXIT say 10+, and younger children usually need help.

Compare the price per day

Divide the price by the number of doors. A LEGO City calendar works out under 80p per day, a tea calendar about 40p, a beauty calendar £2 to £4. A higher price per day is fine if the contents include full size products or a set the child will keep playing with. A cheap calendar with tiny samples is often the worse deal.

Order before 1 December

Some calendars, especially LEGO Star Wars and the beauty brands, regularly sell out in November. Prices tend to drop after the first week of December, but by then the choice is small and you have missed a few days. Order in good time and check the delivery date on Amazon.co.uk.

Think about allergies and diets

Chocolate calendars can contain nuts, milk and soya, and versions of the same brand can differ. Check the allergen list on the box. For children who should eat less sugar, a toy, puzzle or book calendar is a good alternative. Tea calendars are mostly low in caffeine, but check if the recipient is pregnant or sensitive to certain herbs.

Make it a shared ritual

A calendar is more fun when it becomes a moment in the day. Build the LEGO piece together after school, solve the EXIT puzzle after dinner or share a cup of tea. For families with several children, one shared puzzle or building calendar can work better than separate chocolate calendars and saves money too.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best advent calendar in 2026?

Based on contents, price and buyer reviews, the LEGO Star Wars Advent Calendar 2026 75456 is the best all round choice, with 293 pieces and four figures. The LEGO City Advent Calendar 2026 60510 is the best value for children from 5.

Which advent calendar is best for a 4 or 5 year old?

The PLAYMOBIL Dinos Advent Calendar 72225 is labelled 4+ and needs almost no building. The LEGO City Advent Calendar 2026 60510 is labelled 5+ and has simple builds plus lots of Christmas characters.

What is a good advent calendar for adults?

For beauty lovers the Rituals Classic Advent Calendar 2026 and the NIVEA Advent Calendar 2026 are good choices. Puzzle fans enjoy the EXIT advent calendar, and the Pukka tea calendar and the Lindt Lindor calendar are good low priced gifts.

Is a LEGO advent calendar worth it?

Yes, if the child likes LEGO. You get a small build every day and the sets keep their play value after Christmas. At around £18 to £27, LEGO calendars cost about 75p to £1.15 per day.

When should I buy an advent calendar?

Ideally in October or November. Some calendars sell out before December. Prices sometimes drop in December, but by then the choice is limited.

Are toy advent calendars safe for toddlers?

No. LEGO, Playmobil and similar calendars contain small parts and are not suitable for children under 3. Always follow the age the maker prints on the box.
