Buying guide: how to choose the right gifts for teenagers

Look at what they already do

Ask a teenager what they want and you often get a shrug. Look at what they already spend their time on instead. A gamer is happy with a game or a headset, a teenager who photographs everything will use an instant camera or photo printer. A gift that fits an existing hobby almost always gets used after Christmas.

Check which devices they own

Many gifts for teenagers only work with something they already have. A Switch game needs a Switch, a photo printer and a fitness band need a phone with the right app. Check too that they do not already own the same speaker or game. Parents, brothers or sisters can usually tell you quietly.

Plan for running costs

Instax cameras and printers cost roughly 75p per photo in film. Online play on the Switch needs a Nintendo Switch Online subscription and a Kindle only comes alive with books. Add a pack of film, a book gift card or a few months of a subscription, so the teenager can start on Christmas morning.

Choose sturdy and waterproof

Teenagers take their things to school, to the park and on holiday. A speaker rated IP68 or an e-reader rated IPX8 survives rain and a drop in the water. A slightly dearer model that lasts for years is cheaper in the end than a bargain that breaks within months.

Mind the age: 12 is not 17

A 12 year old gets more out of LED lights or Mario Kart, while a 16 year old may prefer earbuds, straighteners or a Kindle. Gaming headsets and games with online chat are worth discussing with parents first. When in doubt, a practical gift they use every day works for every age.

Order early for Christmas

Popular gifts such as the Instax mini 12 and the JBL Flip 7 can sell out in some colours in December. Order in good time and check the delivery date on Amazon.co.uk. Black Friday and the weeks before Christmas often bring lower prices on speakers, earbuds and Kindles.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best gift for a teenager in 2026?

Our top pick is the Fujifilm Instax mini 12 instant camera at around £70. It is easy to use, prints a real photo within minutes and suits both boys and girls. Add a pack of film so they can start right away.

What is a good gift for a teenager under £30?

The Govee RGBIC Wi-Fi LED strip at around £23 is our best value pick and changes the look of a whole bedroom. The Xiaomi Smart Band 9 Active at around £25 is a good choice for a sporty teenager.

What do you buy a teenage boy who games?

Mario Kart 8 Deluxe is a safe choice if he owns a Nintendo Switch. The Razer BlackShark V2 X headset at around £40 works on PC, PS5, Xbox and Switch, so it fits almost every setup.

What do you buy a teenage girl for Christmas?

An Instax camera or photo printer, the JBL Flip 7 speaker, LED lights or the BaByliss Smooth Pro 235 straighteners are all good choices. Look at her hobbies rather than at gender, since most gifts in this list suit any teenager.

Is a Kindle a good gift for a teenager?

Yes, for a teenager who reads. The Kindle Paperwhite has a glare-free screen, lasts up to 12 weeks on a charge and is waterproof. Many UK libraries lend free e-books through Libby, so books do not have to cost much.

How much should you spend on a teenager's Christmas gift?

Most gifts in this list cost between £25 and £100. Bigger items such as a Kindle Paperwhite work well as a shared gift from family. A small extra, such as film or a gift card, makes a cheaper gift feel complete.
