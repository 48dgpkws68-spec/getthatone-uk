Buying guide: how to choose the right air fryers

Single or dual basket?

A single basket is fine for one or two people or for cooking one thing at a time. A dual basket lets you cook two foods at different temperatures and finish them together, which is what makes a full meal possible. For families of four or more, a dual drawer air fryer of 7.6 to 9.5 litres is the most practical choice.

What capacity do you need?

As a rough guide: 3 to 4 litres for one or two people, 5 to 6 litres for two to four, and 7.6 litres or more for families. Remember that food crisps best in a single layer, so a wide basket is often more useful than a deep one.

Is an air fryer cheaper to run than an oven?

Usually, yes. An air fryer uses around 1.5 to 2.7 kW and heats a much smaller space, so it reaches temperature quickly and cooks faster. At current UK energy prices a typical 20 minute air fryer meal costs around 15 to 20p, often less than half the cost of using a full-size electric oven.

Cleaning and coatings

Look for dishwasher-safe drawers and crisper plates. Non-stick coatings last longer if you use wooden or silicone utensils and avoid scouring pads. Several brands, including COSORI and Ninja, now use PFAS-free coatings, which some buyers prefer.

Size on the worktop

Dual drawer air fryers are wide, typically 40 cm or more. Measure your space before buying and leave room at the back for the hot air vent. Stacked models like the Ninja Double Stack are narrower but taller, so check the height under your wall cupboards.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best air fryer in the UK?

The Ninja Foodi MAX Dual Zone AF400UK is the best all-rounder for families at around £169. For a tighter budget, the Tower Vortx T17088 offers dual drawers for under £80.

Which size air fryer should I buy for a family of four?

A dual drawer air fryer of 7.6 litres or more, such as the Ninja AF300UK or AF400UK, lets you cook a main and a side at once for four people.

Can you cook a whole chicken in an air fryer?

Yes, in a single basket of 5.5 litres or more, or in the large drawer of the Tefal Easy Fry Dual XXL. Most dual drawer models with equal drawers are too narrow for a whole chicken, so you may need to spatchcock it.

Do air fryers need oil?

Not much. A teaspoon or a spray of oil helps fresh chips and vegetables crisp up. Frozen foods such as chips and nuggets usually need no extra oil at all.

Are air fryers safe to leave on?

They have automatic shut-off when the timer ends, but like any cooking appliance you should not leave one running unattended for long periods. Keep a few centimetres clear around the vents.
