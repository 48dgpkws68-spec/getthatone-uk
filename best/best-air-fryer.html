The 10 best air fryers of 2026

Short answer (reference prices as of 25 November 2026): our top pick is the Ninja Foodi MAX Dual Zone AF400UK at approx. £169 on Amazon. Best value: Tower Vortx T17088 9L Dual Basket at approx. £79. Cheapest in this top 10: COSORI Lite 3.8L CAF-LI401S at approx. £59.

The air fryer has become a fixture in UK kitchens, and for good reason: it crisps chips, chicken and roast potatoes with a fraction of the oil and heats a much smaller space than a full-size oven. Dual basket models let you cook two things at once and finish them together. How to choose in 5 questions

How many people do you cook for?

For one or two, No. 10 COSORI Lite 3.8L at £59 is rated at 1 to 3 portions. Couples and small families fit No. 4 Ninja AF300UK at £129 with 7.6 L over two drawers, or one big basket in No. 5 COSORI Turbo Blaze 6L at £119. Families of four or more need 9 L or more, such as No. 1 Ninja AF400UK at £169 or No. 2 Tower Vortx at £79.

One basket or two?

Two drawers let you cook a main and a side at different temperatures and finish them together. No. 1 Ninja AF400UK, No. 2 Tower Vortx and No. 3 Ninja Double Stack XL all have a sync finish function. One basket suits cooking one thing at a time: No. 7 Ninja AF160UK at £99 fits 1.4 kg of fries in a body 25 cm wide, and No. 5 COSORI Turbo Blaze has a 6 L basket that fits a whole chicken.

Do you want to cook a whole chicken?

Then look at the maker's chicken figure. No. 1 Ninja AF400UK and No. 7 Ninja AF160UK take a 2 kg chicken, No. 4 Ninja AF300UK one of up to 1.6 kg per drawer and No. 6 Instant Vortex Plus one of 1.6 kg. No. 8 Tefal Easy Fry Dual at £99 fits a whole chicken in its 5.2 L main drawer, with a 3.1 L drawer next to it for a side. A large single basket such as No. 9 Philips Essential Airfryer XL (6.2 L, £109) is the other route.

How much worktop space do you have?

Dual drawer models are wide: No. 1 Ninja AF400UK measures 41.5 cm. No. 3 Ninja Double Stack XL at £219 stacks its two drawers for a width of 28 cm with the same 9.5 L, but it is 38.5 cm tall and 47 cm deep, so check the height under your wall cupboards. For a small kitchen or student flat, No. 10 COSORI Lite 3.8L (27.5 cm wide) and No. 7 Ninja AF160UK (25 cm wide) take the least room.

Do you like to watch your food cook?

No. 6 Instant Vortex Plus ClearCook at £89 has a viewing window and internal light, so you can check the browning without opening the drawer, and OdourErase filters that trap cooking smells. It has a single 5.7 L basket rather than two drawers, and the listing does not confirm dishwasher-safe parts.

When not to buy

  • A side by side dual drawer for a narrow worktop. No. 1 is 41.5 cm wide, No. 8 is 40.2 cm and No. 2 is 38.5 cm. If you have less than about 45 cm free, pick the stacked No. 3 (28 cm wide, but 38.5 cm tall and 47 cm deep) or a single basket such as No. 7 (25 cm wide).
  • A 3.8 litre model for a family. COSORI rates No. 10 at 1 to 3 portions. For four people or more you want 7.6 litres or more over two drawers, such as No. 4 (4 to 6 portions) or No. 1 (up to 8 portions).

Buying guide: how to choose the right air fryers

Single or dual basket?

A single basket is fine for one or two people or for cooking one thing at a time. A dual basket lets you cook two foods at different temperatures and finish them together, which is what makes a full meal possible. For families of four or more, a dual drawer air fryer of 7.6 to 9.5 litres is the most practical choice.

What capacity do you need?

As a rough guide: 3 to 4 litres for one or two people, 5 to 6 litres for two to four, and 7.6 litres or more for families. Litres do not tell the whole story: Which? measures usable capacity in grams of food and found the Ninja AF400UK fits about 2.2 kg. Food crisps best in a single layer, so check the maker's figure for chips or chicken as well as the litres.

Is an air fryer cheaper to run than an oven?

Usually, yes. The models in this list are rated between 1,500 and 2,700 W and heat a much smaller space than an oven. Expert Reviews ran its review models for an hour at 200 °C and measured 0.52 to 1.206 kWh, with an average of 0.74 kWh, which it puts at around 18p at a price cap of 24.67p per kWh. Most meals take far less than an hour: BBC Good Food calculates about 4p for 10 minutes of cooking.

Cleaning and coatings

Look for dishwasher-safe drawers and crisper plates, and check the listing for the exact model: in this list the Tower T17088 is marked hand wash only and Tefal recommends hand washing in its FAQ. Non-stick coatings last longer if you use wooden or silicone utensils and avoid scouring pads. Some newer air fryers advertise ceramic or PFAS-free coatings, but this differs from model to model within a brand.

Size on the worktop

Dual drawer air fryers are wide: 38 cm for the Ninja AF300UK, 38.5 cm for the Tower Vortx, 40.2 cm for the Tefal and 41.5 cm for the Ninja AF400UK. Measure your space before buying and leave room at the back for the hot air vent. The stacked Ninja Double Stack XL is only 28 cm wide but 38.5 cm tall and 47 cm deep, so check the height under your wall cupboards.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best air fryer in the UK?

The Ninja Foodi MAX Dual Zone AF400UK is the best all-rounder for families at around £169. For a tighter budget, the Tower Vortx T17088 offers dual drawers for under £80.

Which size air fryer should I buy for a family of four?

A dual drawer air fryer of 7.6 litres or more, such as the Ninja AF300UK or AF400UK, lets you cook a main and a side at once for four people.

Can you cook a whole chicken in an air fryer?

Yes. Ninja says each drawer of the AF400UK and the single basket of the AF160UK take a 2 kg chicken, and each drawer of the AF300UK takes one of up to 1.6 kg. The large 5.2 litre drawer of the Tefal Easy Fry Dual and the 5.7 litre Instant Vortex Plus (1.6 kg) fit one as well. For a bigger bird, spatchcock it or use the oven.

Do air fryers need oil?

Not much. A teaspoon or a spray of oil helps fresh chips and vegetables crisp up. Frozen foods such as chips and nuggets usually need no extra oil at all.

Are air fryers safe to leave on?

They have automatic shut-off when the timer ends, but like any cooking appliance you should not leave one running unattended for long periods. Keep a few centimetres clear around the vents.

Which air fryer is best for one person?

The COSORI Lite 3.8L at £59 is the easy choice: 27.5 cm wide, 1,500 W, seven presets and a basket COSORI rates at 1 to 3 portions. If you want a Ninja with a single basket, the Ninja Air Fryer MAX AF160UK at £99 has 5.2 L in a body that is 25 cm wide and fits 1.4 kg of fries.

What is the cheapest dual basket air fryer worth buying?

The Tower Vortx T17088 at £79 gives you 9 L over two 4.5 L drawers, 10 presets and Smart Finish, but it is listed as hand wash only. The Tefal Easy Fry Dual at £99 adds a larger 5.2 L main drawer that fits a whole chicken. For the proven Ninja design, the Ninja Foodi Dual Zone AF300UK at £129 has 7.6 L over two drawers.
