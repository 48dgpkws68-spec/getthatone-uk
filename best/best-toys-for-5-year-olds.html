Magic Maths from Orchard Toys is recommended for ages 5 to 7 and is played with 2 to 4 players. Each child gets a wizard board and collects spell ingredients by answering sums. The clever part is the answer check: you rub a heat sensitive patch on the card and the answer appears, so children can see for themselves whether they were right. The game covers addition, subtraction and multiplication and was designed for Key Stage 1 maths. On Amazon.co.uk it scores around 4.7 out of 5 from more than 3,700 buyers, and parents regularly mention that children ask to play it without noticing they are practising sums. For a child who has just turned five, start with the addition cards and leave multiplication for later. Some buyers note that the heat patches react slowly with cold hands. At around £10, it is one of the cheapest useful presents for a five year old.

Buying guide: how to choose the right toys for 5 year olds

What a 5 year old can do

A 5 year old can follow simple rules, wait for a turn and build from picture instructions. Many children start counting and recognising letters at school. That makes this the age for first real LEGO, first board games and craft sets that need more precise hands than toddler toys.

Check the age on the box

Every toy in this guide is labelled for ages 5 and up, or for a range that starts at 5, by the manufacturer. A 5+ label usually says something about difficulty and small parts. For a child who has just turned five, plan to help the first time. Keep small bricks and beads away from younger siblings.

Building, crafting or role play?

Watch what your child does when nobody is steering. Builders are happy with LEGO City, makers with Hama beads, and storytellers with schleich or Playmobil. A 5 year old who never sits still gets more from a scooter or a Stomp Rocket than from a board game.

How much should you spend?

Good toys for a 5 year old start at around £10 for an Orchard Toys game and £14 for a small LEGO City set. Playsets and craft tubs cost £20 to £35. A coding robot or a Maxi Micro scooter is a bigger present of £65 to £150, best saved for a birthday or Christmas.

Boy or girl matters little

At five, children choose by interest. Plenty of girls love monster trucks and rockets, and plenty of boys enjoy beads and horses. Ask what the child is into right now. Open ended toys such as LEGO, beads and figures are a safe choice if you do not know the child well.

Think about batteries, storage and Christmas

Check what is not in the box: Botley needs 5 AAA batteries and a scooter needs a helmet. A tub or case makes tidying easier with beads and bricks. Black Friday falls on 27 November 2026. Order before mid December, and remember that prices on Amazon.co.uk change daily.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best toy for a 5 year old?

Based on specs and parent reviews, the LEGO City Blue Monster Truck 60402 is the best toy for a 5 year old. LEGO labels it 5+, it has 148 pieces and many five year olds can build it alone. The Orchard Toys Magic Maths game is the best value at about £10.

Which toys are suitable for 5 year olds?

Look for toys the manufacturer labels 5+. Good examples are LEGO City and LEGO Friends sets marked 5+, Hama midi beads, Monopoly Junior, schleich Horse Club sets and the Botley 2.0 coding robot. A 5 year old can use all of these, sometimes with a little help at the start.

What is a good educational toy for a 5 year old?

The Orchard Toys Magic Maths game is recommended for ages 5 to 7 and practises sums from Key Stage 1. Botley 2.0, for ages 5 and up, teaches a 5 year old to plan steps in order without a screen. Monopoly Junior practises counting and paying.

Is LEGO suitable for a 5 year old?

Yes. Many LEGO City and LEGO Friends sets are labelled 5+, such as the Blue Monster Truck 60402 and the Creative Beach and Travel Suitcase 42672. They have short builds and picture instructions. Standard LEGO has small parts, so keep it away from children under 3.

What can I buy a 5 year old for under £20?

The Orchard Toys Magic Maths game costs about £10, the LEGO City Blue Monster Truck 60402 around £14, Monopoly Junior around £15 and the LEGO Friends suitcase 42672 around £18. All four are labelled for ages 5 and up.

When should I order Christmas toys for a 5 year old?

Black Friday is on 27 November 2026, a good moment for LEGO and Playmobil deals. Order before mid December to be safe, as popular toys for 5 year olds can sell out, and check the delivery date on Amazon.
