Buying guide: how to choose the right robot vacuum and mops

How good is robot mopping?

There are three types. A fixed pad simply wipes the floor and is fine for dust. Vibrating pads scrub a little harder. Twin spinning pads press down and rotate, which shifts dried-on dirt much better. The best robots also lift their mops or leave them in the dock when they reach carpet, so your rugs stay dry.

Is an all-in-one dock worth it?

A basic dock only empties the bin. An all-in-one dock also washes and dries the mop pads and refills water, so you only empty the dirty water tank and refill the clean one every week or two. Drying with warm air prevents the musty smell of damp pads. For most people it is worth the extra cost.

Suction and pet hair

Suction is quoted in pascals (Pa). Around 7,000 Pa is enough for hard floors and short carpets, while 10,000 Pa and up helps with thick carpets and pet hair. More important for pet owners is an anti-tangle brush, as hair wrapped around a brush cuts performance quickly.

Navigation and obstacle avoidance

LiDAR gives fast, accurate maps and lets you set rooms and no-go zones in the app. An AI camera or 3D sensors on top of that help the robot avoid socks, cables, toys and pet mess. Without a camera you need to tidy the floor before every run.

Running costs

Plan for dust bags, side brushes, filters and mop pads. On most models a year of consumables costs £30 to £60. Bagless docks such as Shark's avoid the cost of bags. Check that spare parts are sold on Amazon.co.uk before you buy a less common brand.

Thresholds and stairs

Most robots climb door thresholds up to about 2 cm. Older UK homes often have higher ones, so check yours. The Dreame X50 can climb up to 6 cm. No robot can climb stairs, but all of them have cliff sensors so they will not fall down them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best robot vacuum and mop in the UK?

The Roborock Qrevo Curv is the best all-rounder for around £599, with strong suction and a dock that washes and dries its mops. The Eufy X10 Pro Omni is the best value at about £399.

Do robot vacuums work on carpet?

Yes. Models with 10,000 Pa or more and a good brush clean carpets well. Choose a robot that lifts or detaches its mops so carpets do not get wet.

Are robot vacuums good for pet hair?

The best ones are, especially those with anti-tangle brushes like the Roborock Qrevo Curv, Dreame L40 Ultra and eufy C28 Omni. A self-emptying dock is useful because pet hair fills the bin quickly.

Can a robot mop replace a normal mop?

For regular cleaning, mostly yes. Spinning pads handle day-to-day dirt and light stains. For very sticky spills or deep cleaning grout you will still occasionally need a manual mop.

How long does a robot vacuum last?

With regular replacement of brushes and filters, three to five years is typical. The battery is often the first part to wear and can usually be replaced.
