Buying guide: how to choose the right gifts for him under £100

Start with what he already does

The best gift fits a habit he already has. A commuter gets more from noise cancelling headphones, a reader from a Kindle and a barbecue fan from a Weber. A gift for a hobby he does not have yet often ends up in a cupboard, however good the product is.

Spend the budget on one good thing

Under £100 you can afford one product from a well-known brand instead of three cheaper items. Brands such as Sony, Braun, Philips, LEGO and Weber have plenty of buyer reviews and warranties that are easy to claim. That gives you confidence when you do not know the product yourself.

Check it suits his devices

The Sony headphones, the Amazfit watch and the soundcore speaker work with both Android and iPhone. The Hi-Res wireless sound of the Motion 300 works best with a compatible Android phone. The Kindle needs an Amazon account, which most people already have.

Count in running costs and extras

A OneBlade needs a new blade every few months and the Braun shaver has a 2-pin plug, so check he has a shaver socket or an adapter. A barbecue needs charcoal and the Kindle version at £94.99 shows ads on the lock screen. Count these in, or give the extra with the gift.

Christmas, Black Friday and delivery

Black Friday is on 27 November 2026 and Cyber Monday follows on 30 November. Prices can move then, but there is no guarantee. For Christmas it is best to order by early December. Amazon usually extends its returns window for gifts bought in the run-up to Christmas, so check the dates on the product page.

Watch the law on knives

In the UK a folding knife with a locking blade, such as the Leatherman Skeletool, may not be carried in public without a good reason. That is fine for a toolbox, a car or a campsite, but tell him. You must be 18 to buy one.
