The 10 best energy efficient electric heaters of 2026

With energy prices still high, many UK households heat just the room they are in rather than running the central heating for the whole house. A portable electric heater makes that easy, but there is a lot of confusion about which type is cheapest to run. Every electric heater turns almost all of its electricity into heat; the savings come from a good thermostat, a timer and choosing the right type for your room. Buying guide: how to choose the right energy efficient electric heaters

Are some electric heaters more efficient?

All plug-in electric heaters convert close to 100 percent of their electricity into heat, so one 2 kW heater uses the same energy as another per hour on full power. The real savings come from a precise thermostat, a timer and heating only the room or the person that needs it. A heater that switches off as soon as the room is warm costs less to run than one that keeps going.

Which type suits your room?

Oil-filled radiators are slow to warm up but give steady, silent heat and keep radiating after they switch off, which suits living rooms and bedrooms used for several hours. Oil-free micathermic and convector heaters warm up faster. Fan heaters are best for short bursts in small rooms or for warming yourself at a desk.

What does it cost to run?

At a UK electricity price of around 27p per kWh, a 2 kW heater on full power costs about 54p an hour. On a lower setting or with the thermostat cycling it can be half that. Heating one room this way is often cheaper than running gas central heating for the whole house, but more expensive per unit of heat than gas.

Thermostat and timer

A digital thermostat that shows degrees is more accurate than a numbered dial and avoids overheating. A 24 hour timer lets you warm a room before you get up or come home without leaving the heater on all day. Smart heaters add app control and weekly schedules.

Safety

Look for a tip-over switch and overheat cut-out, which all heaters in this list have. Keep heaters at least a metre from curtains, furniture and bedding, never cover them, and plug them directly into a wall socket rather than an extension lead, as a 2 kW heater draws a lot of current.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest electric heater to run in the UK?

Per hour, a lower-wattage heater costs less, but all electric heaters are roughly equally efficient. The cheapest to run in practice is one with a good thermostat and timer, such as the De'Longhi Dragon 4, used to heat only the room you are in.

Is an oil-filled radiator cheaper than a fan heater?

Per kWh they cost the same. An oil-filled radiator is better for heating a room for hours because it holds heat and cycles off. A fan heater is better for short bursts, as it heats quickly and you only use it while you are there.

Is it cheaper to use an electric heater than central heating?

If you only need to heat one room, usually yes. Heating the whole house with gas is cheaper per unit of heat, but heating empty rooms wastes energy. Many households use a portable heater in the living room in the evening and keep the central heating lower.

Can I leave an electric heater on overnight?

Oil-filled radiators with a thermostat and safety cut-out are designed for long use, but it is safer to use a timer. Never leave fan heaters running unattended, and never cover any heater.

What size heater do I need?

As a rule of thumb, allow around 100 W per square metre for a reasonably insulated room. A 2 kW heater suits a room of about 15 to 20 square metres. Larger or draughty rooms may need 2.5 kW.
