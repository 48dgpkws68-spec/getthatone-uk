Buying guide: how to choose the right LEGO sets for kids

Start with the age on the box

LEGO prints a minimum age on every box, such as 4+, 7+ or 9+. It tells you how complex the build is, not how grown up the theme is. Sets for under 3s are DUPLO; regular LEGO has small parts and is not suitable for children under 3. A child with lots of LEGO experience can often handle a set one step up, but a first set is best bought at the stated age.

DUPLO, Classic or a theme?

DUPLO has big bricks for children from 18 months to about 4 or 5. Classic boxes contain loose bricks without a fixed model, ideal for free building. Themes such as City, Friends, Minecraft, Star Wars and Harry Potter give you a model with instructions and characters. All regular LEGO bricks fit together, whatever the theme.

Play set or display model

Some sets are made to play with after the build: they have minifigures, vehicles and moving parts. Others, such as the Creator 3in1 animals, are mostly about building and look good on a shelf. Think about what your child does with finished sets. A child who acts out stories gets more from a play set with characters.

What do you pay per brick?

A rough rule is that licensed themes such as Star Wars and Harry Potter cost more per brick than LEGO's own themes. Classic boxes are the cheapest per piece. Piece count is not everything, though: one large part counts the same as one tiny stud. Compare the size of the finished model and the number of minifigures as well.

Order in time for Christmas

LEGO prices on Amazon.co.uk move a lot, and discounts of 20 to 30 percent on the list price are common around Black Friday. Popular sets can sell out in December. Check that the listing is for the single set and not a bundle, and that it is sold new.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best LEGO set for kids in 2026?

Based on specs and buyer reviews, the LEGO Friends Friendship Tree House Hangout 42652 is the best all round set for kids from 8. It has 701 pieces, four characters and lots of play functions. The Classic Large Creative Brick Box 10698 is the best value.

From what age can a child play with LEGO?

DUPLO is made for children from 18 months. Regular LEGO starts with 4+ sets, which have larger starter pieces and simple instructions. Regular LEGO contains small parts and is not suitable for children under 3.

What does the age on a LEGO box mean?

It is the minimum age at which LEGO expects a child to build the set without much help. It is about difficulty, not content. An 8+ set has more pieces and smaller steps than a 6+ set.

Which LEGO set is good for a child who has never had LEGO?

A Classic box such as the 10698 is a safe start from age 4, because there is no right or wrong way to build. For a child of 6 or older, a small City set such as the Holiday Adventure Camper Van 60454 is a good first model with instructions.

Do sets from different themes fit together?

Yes. All regular LEGO bricks fit together, whether they come from City, Friends, Minecraft or Star Wars. DUPLO bricks also fit on regular LEGO bricks. Technic uses pins and beams, but those connect to regular bricks too.

What is a good LEGO set for under £20?

The City Holiday Adventure Camper Van 60454, the Minecraft Armadillo Mine Expedition 21269 and the Technic Monster Jam ThunderROARus 42200 all usually sell for around £15 to £20 on Amazon.co.uk.
