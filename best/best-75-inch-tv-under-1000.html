Buying guide: how to choose the right 75-inch TV under £1000

What does £1000 buy in a 75-inch TV?

In 2026 a £1000 budget covers most mainstream 75-inch 4K TVs on Amazon.co.uk. Around £600 to £700 you find the QLED sets from TCL and Hisense and Samsung's basic Crystal UHD. Between £700 and £900 come the 144 Hz TCL 75T8C-UK, the Philips with Ambilight, the Samsung QLED and the LG QNED. Mini LED starts at the very top of the budget with the Hisense 75U7QTUK. OLED at this size costs far more.

75 inch or 65 inch?

A 75-inch TV is roughly 167 cm wide and close to a metre tall, about 22 cm wider than a 65-inch set. It suits a viewing distance of around 2.5 to 3.5 metres. The trade-off is picture quality per pound: for the price of a basic 75-inch screen you can buy a 65-inch Mini LED. Our guide to the best 65-inch TV under £1000 lists those. If size matters most, the ten TVs here are the ones to compare.

QLED, Mini LED or basic LED?

A big screen makes weak contrast more visible, so panel type matters more at 75 inches. QLED adds quantum dots for richer colour, and seven of the ten TVs here have it. Mini LED, as in the Hisense 75U7QTUK, adds local dimming for darker blacks and brighter highlights. Basic LED sets such as the Samsung UE75U7020H and LG 75UA75006LA are fine for daytime TV and sport, but look greyer in a dark room.

Gaming on a 75-inch TV

Only two TVs in this guide have a 144 Hz panel: the TCL 75T8C-UK and the Hisense 75U7QTUK. Both support VRR and suit a PS5 or Xbox Series X at 120 frames per second. We list the other eight as 60 Hz sets. Do not be misled by terms such as 120 Hz Game Accelerator in a product title, as on the TCL 75P7K-UK: that is not the native refresh rate.

Delivery, stand and wall mounting

A 75-inch TV comes in a box around 1.8 metres long, so check your hallway, stairs and car before you order. Most sets stand on two feet near the edges, which needs a unit almost as wide as the screen. For the wall you need a bracket rated for the weight and the right VESA size: the Philips uses 400 x 300 mm and the LG 75UA75006LA 300 x 300 mm. Unboxing is a job for two people.

Prices move around

TV prices on Amazon.co.uk change often, especially around Black Friday and in January. The Hisense 75U7QTUK is listed at £999, so a small rise puts it over the limit of this guide, and the Samsung QE75Q7FAAU is offered far above £1000 by some sellers. Check the current price and who sells it before you buy. If a pick goes over budget, the TCL 75T8C-UK and Hisense 75A7QTUK stay comfortably below it.
