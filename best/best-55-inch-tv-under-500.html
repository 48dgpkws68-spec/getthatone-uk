Buying guide: how to choose the right 55-inch TV under £500

What does £500 buy in a 55-inch TV?

In 2026 a £500 budget gets you Mini LED, which used to be a premium feature. Mini LED TVs such as the TCL 55C6K-UK use many small LEDs behind the screen that dim and brighten in zones, so HDR looks brighter and blacks deeper. Below £400 you mostly find QLED and basic LED models: fine for a bedroom, less impressive for films. OLED does not fit this budget at 55 inches; see our full 55-inch TV guide for those.

Mini LED, QLED or LED?

Mini LED gives the most contrast and brightness under £500 and is our first choice for a living room. QLED adds a quantum dot layer for richer colour but usually has no local dimming, so dark scenes look greyer; the Amazon Fire TV Omni QLED is an exception with local dimming. A standard LED TV such as the Samsung UE55U7020H is the cheapest option and fine for the news, soaps and a second room.

Refresh rate for gaming

With a PS5, Xbox Series X or gaming PC, look for a native 120 Hz or 144 Hz panel with VRR and ALLM. Under £500 only the TCL 55C6K-UK and TCL 55T8C-UK qualify here, both at 144 Hz. The other TVs in this list run at 60 Hz, which limits consoles to 60 frames per second. A high motion rate on the box is not the same as a native refresh rate.

Dolby Vision or HDR10+?

Netflix, Disney+ and Apple TV+ mostly stream in Dolby Vision, while Prime Video also uses HDR10+. TCL, Hisense and the Amazon Omni support both. Samsung supports HDR10+ only, and the LG QNED models support neither, only HDR10 and HLG. Brightness matters more than the format: a dim TV gets little out of either.

Smart platform and Freely

Google TV (TCL) has the biggest app choice and Chromecast. Tizen (Samsung) and webOS (LG) are polished and have every UK catch-up app. VIDAA (Hisense) is quick and includes Freely, which combines live TV and catch-up in one guide. Titan OS (Philips) and Fire TV (Amazon) show more promotions. A streaming stick can always replace a weak platform.

Prices move around

TV prices on Amazon.co.uk change often, especially around Black Friday. The TCL 55C6K-UK and Amazon Fire TV Omni QLED sit close to £500 and can climb above it between sales. Check the current price before you buy. If a pick goes over £500, the TCL 55T8C-UK and Hisense 55E7STUK give you most of the picture for less.
