Buying guide: how to choose the right 32-inch TV under £200

What does £200 buy in a 32-inch TV?

In 2026 a budget of £200 covers almost every mainstream 32-inch TV on Amazon.co.uk. Between about £120 and £160 you find HD Ready sets from LG, Sharp and Xiaomi, plus the Full HD TCL 32V5C-UK and Hisense 32A4QTUK. Between £160 and £200 come the Samsung H5000, the Amazon Fire TV 2-Series and the Full HD QLED sets from Hisense and TCL. Samsung's 32-inch QLED costs around £300, so it falls outside this guide.

HD Ready or Full HD?

HD Ready means 1366 x 768 pixels, about one million in total. Full HD means 1920 x 1080, about two million. From three metres away the difference on a 32-inch screen is small. From a metre and a half, at a desk or in a small bedroom, Full HD shows clearly sharper text, menus and sport. Four TVs in this guide are Full HD: the Hisense 32E5QTUK and 32A4QTUK and the TCL 32V5D-UK and 32V5C-UK. No 32-inch TV here is 4K.

QLED or plain LED?

QLED adds a quantum dot layer to an LED panel, which gives richer and more accurate colour. Four TVs here have it: the Hisense 32E5QTUK, the two TCL sets and the Xiaomi TV F Pro 32. Do not expect strong HDR from any of them. The listed brightness ranges from about 200 to 400 nits, and no set has local dimming. For daytime TV, news and catch-up a plain LED set such as the Hisense 32A4QTUK is fine.

Which smart platform?

Fire TV, on the TCL 32V5D-UK, the Amazon and both Xiaomi sets, has a very wide app choice and Alexa. Android TV on the TCL 32V5C-UK adds Google Cast. VIDAA on the two Hisense sets is quick and includes Freely. Roku on the Sharp is the simplest to use. Tizen on the Samsung and webOS on the LG are polished but come with HD Ready panels. Freely brings live BBC, ITV, Channel 4 and Channel 5 over wifi without an aerial.

HDMI ports and sound

Most 32-inch TVs under £200 have two HDMI ports. That covers a set-top box and a console, or a console and a soundbar, but not all three. The Sharp 32HD2325K and the Amazon Fire TV 2-Series have three. The built-in speakers are small, typically 8 to 10 W in total, so voices are clear but bass is thin. A basic soundbar on the HDMI ARC port helps a lot for films.

Prices move around

TV prices on Amazon.co.uk change often, especially around Black Friday and in January. The Hisense 32E5QTUK and TCL 32V5D-UK sit within about £10 of the £200 limit, and the Amazon Fire TV 2-Series has been listed at £199.99. Check the current price and who sells it before you buy. If a pick goes over budget, the TCL 32V5C-UK and Hisense 32A4QTUK stay well below the limit and are both Full HD.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best 32-inch TV under £200?

The Hisense 32E5QTUK. It is a Full HD QLED with Dolby Atmos, Freely and two HDMI ports, for around £189 on Amazon.co.uk, and more than 1,800 buyers rate it 4.3 out of 5.

Is Full HD worth it on a 32-inch TV?

Yes if you sit closer than about two metres or use the screen with a laptop or console. Full HD has 1920 x 1080 pixels against 1366 x 768 for HD Ready. From across a large room the difference is small.

What is the cheapest good 32-inch TV?

The TCL 32V5C-UK at around £127 is the best value: a Full HD QLED with Android TV. The Xiaomi TV F 32 is cheaper still at about £119, but it is an HD Ready LED set.

Which 32-inch TV under £200 has Freely?

The Hisense 32E5QTUK and 32A4QTUK and the Sharp 32HD2325K have Freely built in. One listing for the TCL 32V5D-UK also names it. Freely brings live TV and catch-up over the internet without an aerial.

How many HDMI ports does a 32-inch TV have?

Usually two. In this guide the Sharp 32HD2325K and the Amazon Fire TV 32-inch 2-Series have three HDMI ports. The other sets list two, and the Xiaomi TV F Pro 32 listing does not state the number.

Can you get a 4K 32-inch TV under £200?

Not among the mainstream models in this guide. Under £200 the choice is between HD Ready and Full HD. At 32 inches Full HD is sharp enough for normal viewing distances.
