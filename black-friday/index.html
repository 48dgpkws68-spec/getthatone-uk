Three tips for Black Friday

Choose your model in advance

Read the guide now and note the model you want and today's price. On Black Friday you will see straight away whether a deal is worth it.

Compare with recent prices

A discount off a list price says little. Compare with the price of the past few weeks instead.

Check the seller

On Amazon, see whether a product is sold by Amazon itself or by a marketplace seller. That affects delivery, returns and warranty.
