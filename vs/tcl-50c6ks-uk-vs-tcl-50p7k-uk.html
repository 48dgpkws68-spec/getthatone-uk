TCL 50C6KS-UK vs TCL 50P7K-UK

The TCL 50C6KS-UK and the TCL 50P7K-UK are both 50-inch 60 Hz TVs under £400 with quantum dot colour, Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and Google TV. The difference is the backlight: the 50C6KS has QD-Mini LED with local dimming, while the 50P7K is a plain QLED without local dimming that costs clearly less.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Buy the TCL 50C6KS-UK if you watch films and HDR series in the evening: it is the only Mini LED set at this size and budget. Choose the TCL 50P7K-UK if you want good colour and Google TV for the least money. Both are 60 Hz, so neither suits 120 Hz console gaming.
