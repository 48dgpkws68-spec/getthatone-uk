Verdict

Our conclusion

The Ninja Foodi MAX AF400UK is the better air fryer, with more even browning and a sturdier coating, and it is the one to buy if you cook for four or more every day. The Tower Vortx T17088 is the better deal if you want dual drawers without paying Ninja prices and don't mind shaking the food halfway through. Both are wide, so measure your worktop.
