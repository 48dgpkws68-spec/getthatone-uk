TCL 55P7K-UK vs TCL 50V6C-UK

The TCL 55P7K-UK and the TCL 50V6C-UK are both 4K Google TVs from TCL that sit under £300, so the choice comes down to size, panel and budget. The 55P7K is a 55 inch QLED with Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and a 120Hz Game Accelerator mode. The 50V6C is a 50 inch set with a standard LED panel that costs noticeably less.
