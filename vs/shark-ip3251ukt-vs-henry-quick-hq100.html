Verdict

Our conclusion

Choose the Shark PowerDetect Clean & Empty if you have pets or a busy family home and want strong suction with minimal emptying, and have space for the dock. Choose the Henry Quick if you prefer bags, want something light and trust the Henry name, and live in a smaller home. Expect to trim hair off the Henry's brushroll now and then.
