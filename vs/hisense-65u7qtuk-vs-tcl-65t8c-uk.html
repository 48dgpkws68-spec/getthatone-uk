Hisense 65U7QTUK vs TCL 65T8C-UK

The Hisense 65U7QTUK and the TCL 65T8C-UK are both 65-inch TVs under £1000 with a 144 Hz panel, VRR, Dolby Vision and HDR10+. The difference is the backlight: the Hisense has Mini LED with full array local dimming, while the TCL is a QLED without local dimming that costs well under £500.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Buy the Hisense 65U7QTUK if picture quality comes first: Mini LED with local dimming makes films and HDR look clearly better on a 65-inch screen. Choose the TCL 65T8C-UK if you mainly game or want to keep money aside for a soundbar, since it keeps 144 Hz, Dolby Vision and Google TV for less.
