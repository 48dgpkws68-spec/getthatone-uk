Our conclusion

Pick the Meaco Arete One if it will run in living spaces or bedrooms and you value low noise, a HEPA filter and a five year warranty. Pick the Pro Breeze if you mainly want full 20 litre capacity and a bigger tank for less, and do not mind more noise on high. For most homes the Meaco is the better long term buy.
