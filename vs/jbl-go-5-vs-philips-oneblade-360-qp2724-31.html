Verdict

Our conclusion

Choose the JBL Go 5 if you want a safe gift that suits nearly every man, especially one who listens to music on the go. Choose the Philips OneBlade 360 QP2724/31 if he has a beard or stubble: it is the more practical everyday tool and the better value. Keep in mind that the OneBlade needs a new blade every few months.
