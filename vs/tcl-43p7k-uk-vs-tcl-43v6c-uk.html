TCL 43P7K-UK vs TCL 43V6C-UK

The TCL 43P7K-UK and the TCL 43V6C-UK are both 43-inch 4K TVs under £300 with Google TV and a 60 Hz panel. The difference is the picture: the 43P7K is a QLED with Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and Dolby Atmos, while the 43V6C is a basic LED set with Dolby Audio that costs clearly less.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Buy the TCL 43P7K-UK if this is your main TV or you watch a lot of HDR films and series: it has QLED colour, Dolby Vision and HDR10+. Choose the TCL 43V6C-UK if you want a 4K Google TV for a second room at the lowest price. Both are 60 Hz, so neither suits 120 Hz console gaming.
