Comparison

Days of Wonder Ticket to Ride Europe vs Blue Orange Kingdomino

Both games are labelled 8+ and are easy to explain, but they fill a very different evening. Ticket to Ride Europe is a route building game for 2 to 5 players that takes 30 to 60 minutes. Kingdomino is a tile laying game for 2 to 4 players that is over in about 15 minutes and costs clearly less.
