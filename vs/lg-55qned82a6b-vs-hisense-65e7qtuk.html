Verdict

Our conclusion

Choose the LG 55QNED82A6B if you want a dependable 55 inch TV from a big brand with the best software at this price. Choose the Hisense 65E7QTUK if screen size and Dolby Vision matter more to you and you can live with VIDAA's smaller app store. Both are 60 Hz sets with average contrast in a dark room, so neither is a gaming or home cinema specialist.
