De'Longhi Dragon 4 TRDX40820E vs Mill Gentle Air WiFi OIL2000WIFI3 2000W

The De'Longhi Dragon 4 and the Mill Gentle Air WiFi are both 2 kW oil filled radiators with three heat settings, so they warm a room equally well. The choice comes down to control: the Dragon 4 has a classic thermostat and a 24 hour timer, while the Mill adds an electronic thermostat in 0.1 °C steps, weekly schedules in an app and a 5 year warranty. That comfort costs about £80 more.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Buy the De'Longhi Dragon 4 if you want a dependable oil radiator for one room and are happy with a dial and a daily timer: it gives you the same heat for less money. Choose the Mill Gentle Air WiFi if you want precise temperature control, weekly schedules and phone control, or if the longer 5 year warranty matters to you. For most UK homes the Dragon 4 is the smarter buy.
