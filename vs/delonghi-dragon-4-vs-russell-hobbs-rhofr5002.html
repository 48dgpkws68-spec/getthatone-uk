Verdict

Our conclusion

Buy the De'Longhi Dragon 4 if you will use the heater daily in a living room and want the timer and more precise control to keep running costs down. If you need occasional heat in a bedroom or home office, the Russell Hobbs is the cheaper way to get silent oil-filled warmth. You can add a plug-in timer to the Russell Hobbs if you miss that feature.
