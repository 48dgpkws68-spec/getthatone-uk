Amazon Fire HD 8 Kids (2024) vs Amazon Fire 7 Kids (2022) 16 GB

The Amazon Fire HD 8 Kids (2024) and the Amazon Fire 7 Kids (2022) 16 GB are our best overall and best value tablets for kids. Amazon labels both for ages 3 to 7, and both come with a Kid-Proof Case, a year of Amazon Kids+ and a 2-year worry-free guarantee. The HD 8 Kids has a bigger and sharper screen with more storage, the Fire 7 Kids is smaller, lighter and about £40 cheaper.
