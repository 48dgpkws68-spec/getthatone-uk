Sony WF-1000XM6 vs Soundcore Liberty 4 NC

Verdict

Our conclusion

Buy the Sony WF-1000XM6 if you travel a lot or care about sound quality and the best noise cancelling, and don't mind paying for it. If you want a good all-rounder without the flagship price, the Soundcore Liberty 4 NC is an easy recommendation, with the case and bass tuning being the main compromises. You can tame its bass in the app.
