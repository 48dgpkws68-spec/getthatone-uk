LG C5 OLED55C54LA vs Samsung S90F QE55S90FAEXXU

The LG C5 and the Samsung S90F are both 55 inch OLEDs with 144 Hz and four HDMI 2.1 ports, so UK buyers often can't decide between them. The big differences are the panel and the HDR format: LG uses an OLED evo panel with Dolby Vision, whereas Samsung's QD-OLED is brighter and more colourful but skips Dolby Vision. The Samsung is also noticeably cheaper.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Buy the LG C5 if you want Dolby Vision and the cleaner smart TV interface and are happy to pay extra for a proven all-rounder. If you want the most OLED for your money and watch plenty of daytime TV, the Samsung S90F is the smarter buy. Gamers will be happy with either one, as both have four HDMI 2.1 ports.
