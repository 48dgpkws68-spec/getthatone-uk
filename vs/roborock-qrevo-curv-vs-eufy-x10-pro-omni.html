Roborock Qrevo Curv vs Eufy X10 Pro Omni

Both the Roborock Qrevo Curv and the Eufy X10 Pro Omni come with docks that empty the bin, wash the mops and dry them. The Roborock has stronger suction and better rug handling, while the Eufy gets you the same hands-off routine for around two-thirds of the price.

Verdict

Our conclusion

The Roborock Qrevo Curv is the better robot for homes with rugs, pets or lots of carpet and is worth paying more for. The Eufy X10 Pro Omni is the pick for mostly hard floors and a tighter budget, since it covers daily cleaning with a full dock for noticeably less. Either way, expect to move the odd loose cable.
