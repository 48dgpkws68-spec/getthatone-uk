Verdict

Our conclusion

Pick the Dreame X40 Ultra Complete if your pet has the occasional accident or you hate tidying the floor before each run, as its camera avoids pet mess, cables and bowls. Pick the Roborock Qrevo S Pro if you want strong suction and a complete dock for less, and you clear the floor first. For most pet homes the Dreame is the safer buy.
