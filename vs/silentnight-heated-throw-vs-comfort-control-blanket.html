Verdict

Our conclusion

Get the Silentnight Luxury Heated Throw if your goal is to turn the heating down and stay warm on the sofa, since it is soft, flexible and washable. Get the Comfort Control Electric Blanket if you want a pre-warmed double bed for very little money. Note that couples share one control on the blanket, so a dual control model suits two people with different needs.
